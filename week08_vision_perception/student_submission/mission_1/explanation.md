# mission_1

## diagnosis
For normal_1, the box is positioned on the mug. The green box captures the mug without cropping part of it, and the candidate contours show blue lines around different objects including the mug. The detector was able to select the mug correctly in normal lighting. The hue was set to 0 to 35, saturation to 70 to 255 and value to 26 to180, which allowed the mug's brown color to pass the threshold mask. However, some of the desk also passed the color rule because it has a similar brown color.  For  dim_1, the box is postioned on the enrire frame which includes the mug as well. the candidate contours show blue lines around different objects including the mug with more depth than the seelcor normal_1 however the threshold mask has most of the table and some part of the mug white which means it didnt have an exact match of the mug color. For dim_1, the box covers the entire frame, including the mug, which means the detector did not correctly locate the mug. The candidate contours show blue lines around different objects, including the mug and parts of the desk. The raw threshold mask shows that most of the table and only some parts of the mug are white, meaning those pixels passed the color rule. Since the lighting is dimmer, the mug's appearance changes which makes it harder for the detector to separate it from the background using the same settings. 

## limitations
I changed the hue range, and I noticed that the green box shifted away from the mug as I increased the hue range. The raw threshold mask also had fewer white areas in the frame. The detector ended up selecting the table instead of the mug because the color range was not matching the mug correctly. One limitation is that the detector might still confuse the mug with a different cup or background that has similar colors.

## Response

This activity gave me a better understanding of how robots plan routes and navigate around obstacles while considering the people around them. I learned that finding the shortest path does not always mean it is the best or safest option. What stood out to me was how different planners performed depending on the map and the settings used. For example, in mission 1, A* found the same path length as BFS but expanded fewer nodes. In mission 3, increasing the repulsion strength did not always improve the path because some conditions caused the planner to get stuck or fail. The activity also made me more interested in learning how to improve robot navigation in real world environments. I think it is important to consider safety and personal space when designing robots that share environments with people. Since our tests were done in simulation, the results may not be exactly the same as what would happen with a real robot. Overall, this activity helped me understand that robot navigation is not just about reaching a destination but also choosing a route that is efficient, reliable, and safe for the people around it.

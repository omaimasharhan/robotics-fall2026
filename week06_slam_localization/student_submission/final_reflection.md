## Response

This activity gave me a better understanding of how robots use LiDAR and SLAM to map their surroundings and how the robot's movement can affect the accuracy of the map. I learned that when a robot moves through an environment, it uses sensor measurements and estimates of its position to build a map. However, errors in its estimated position can cause walls to appear as multiple lines or make some areas of the map inaccurate. It also showed me that the route the robot takes can affect how much of the environment it maps and how long it takes to complete the task. What stood out to me was that a map can have better numerical results but still not show the overall layout as clearly as another map. Comparing Mission 1 and Mission 2 helped me understand the tradeoff between mapping more space in less time and producing a clearer map. Overall, this activity helped me understand why accurate mapping and localization are important for a robot to navigate safely. It also motivated me to learn more about SLAM and how to improve a robot's mapping process so it can understand its environment and complete tasks more effectively.

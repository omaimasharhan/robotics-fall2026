Working through all three missions, I learned that giving instructions to a robot needs to be precise and descriptive. In mission 1 adding instructions for the robot to explain its limitations helped make the response more useful when it could not pick up the blue cup. In mission 2 asking it to identify the measuring tape and describe its visible features made the answer more specific. In mission 3 even though I gave the robot step by step directions it only made 12 successful moves out of 50 and never reached the goal. This showed me that clear prompts and visible evidence can improve responses, but they do not guarantee that the robot will follow instructions correctly. One limitation is that the model can still make incorrect decisions when choosing actions.

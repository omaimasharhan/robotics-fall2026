This activity gave me a better understanding of how foundation models can be used to give instructions to robots and guide their actions. I learned that prompts need to be clear and specific because the model does not always understand what we want it to do. What stood out to me was how changing the wording of a request affected the model's responses. For example, in mission 1 adding instructions to explain its limitations made the response more useful when it could not pick up the blue cup. In mission 2 asking the model to identify the measuring tape and describe its visible features helped it give a more specific answer. However, in mission 3 even with detailed step by step directions the robot struggled to follow the route and was unable to reach the goal. This showed me that a detailed prompt does not always guarantee the robot will follow the instructions correctly. The activity made me more interested in learning how to improve the way robots understand instructions and interact with their surroundings. I also think it is important to make sure robots act safely and explain their limitations instead of making incorrect decisions. Overall, this activity helped me understand that getting useful robot behavior involves more than just giving instructions. We also need to check the model's responses and make sure its actions match what we asked it to do.
